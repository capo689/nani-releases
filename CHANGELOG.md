# What's new in Nani Accounting

## 0.5.0 (launch)

**Autopilot for your AI**
- Turn it on in Suggested by AI and your AI's routine suggestions apply themselves: categorizing new transactions, answering Interview questions and adding notes, each up to a limit you set (default $2,000).
- Everything else still waits for you: rules, journal entries, reversals, new accounts, invoices, payments, anything over your limit and anything in a locked period.
- "What Autopilot did" lists every change, with Undo, Undo today's and Undo all. Year end asks you to look it over once.
- Account and card numbers in bank descriptions are masked to their last four digits before anything reaches an AI.

**Year end in one place**
- **Wrap up your year:** one screen lists everything left to finish, grouped as Needs you, Please confirm, Worth a look and Done, with a link to fix each item. It checks 26 things, from uncategorized lines and missing statements to unpaid payroll taxes and missing W-9s.
- **The CPA package:** one button makes a folder and a single ZIP with a Start here page, every report in PDF and Excel, trial balance files for tax software, the general ledger, QuickBooks files, the 1099 list, the mileage log, your filed documents and a fingerprint of every file. A draft can be made any time.
- **Lock the year** with one button when you're done.

**More of your business**
- Splits: one charge across several accounts, and the same split for every matching charge.
- Loan payments split into interest and principal the way the lender does it.
- Journal entries and recurring entries, and classes for locations, jobs or lines of business.
- Bills you owe, with due dates and aging.
- Mileage log at the IRS rate for each trip's date, the home office deduction, budgets, sales tax by quarter, and year-end inventory.
- Partners: guaranteed payments per partner, and ownership changes during the year.
- S and C corporations: officer pay, Schedules M-1 and M-2, and checks on distributions.
- 1099s: full contractor details from W-9s, the new $2,000 limit for 2026, and backup withholding warnings.

**Better at the basics**
- Nani learns: put a merchant in the same place twice and it does it from then on, and "Remember for next time" in the Interview now catches the same merchant on the next statement.
- Your rules, in one list on Transactions, with Pause and Delete. A matched transfer can be unmatched.
- Transactions shows the newest first, with Show more.
- Overlapping downloads, even a QFX and a CSV of the same month, no longer double count.
- 18 reports, including cash flow, the return summary and budget vs. actual.

**Launch**

## 0.3.0 (October 13, 2026)
- Invoices: make them in Nani, with your logo and address on top, numbered automatically, and added to Who owes you.
- Your company: add your address, contact details and logo once (Accounts and owners); they go on invoices and exported reports.
- Every report exports to PDF (on your letterhead) and CSV.
- Transactions: sort by date, description or amount, select many (or everything shown) and categorize them at once, and optionally remember it as a rule.
- Home: "How the year is going": income, spending, profit and margin against last year, a 12-month profit chart, what stands out, your biggest expenses, and cash on hand. Your AI can explain it in plain English.
- Suggested by AI shows whether your AI is connected and when it last looked, with a ready request to paste into Claude Desktop.
- The Inbox explains its two ways to add files more clearly.
- Text boxes, dropdowns and buttons line up at the same height.
