# What's new in Nani Accounting

## 0.3.0 (October 13, 2026)
- Invoices: make them in Nani, with your logo and address on top, numbered automatically, and added to Who owes you.
- Your company: add your address, contact details and logo once (Accounts and owners); they go on invoices and exported reports.
- Every report exports to PDF (on your letterhead) and CSV.
- Transactions: sort by date, description or amount, select many (or everything shown) and categorize them at once, and optionally remember it as a rule.
- Home: "How the year is going": income, spending, profit and margin against last year, a 12-month profit chart, what stands out, your biggest expenses, and cash on hand. Your AI can explain it in plain English.
- Suggested by AI shows whether your AI is connected and when it last looked, with a ready request to paste into Claude Desktop.
- The Inbox explains its two ways to add files more clearly.
- Text boxes, dropdowns and buttons line up at the same height.

## 0.2.0 (October 12, 2026)
- First public beta: the Inbox (drop in statements, invoices, receipts, W-9s and tax letters), Who owes you, automatic updates, and the Acme Anvil Co. sample books.
